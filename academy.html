🎓 The Data House Academy

Empowering the Next Generation of Data Science & AI

At The Data House Academy, we are on a mission to transform Sri Lanka’s youth into tomorrow’s data science innovators. We believe the journey into Data Science and Artificial Intelligence can begin as early as age 16, right after O/Ls. That’s why our academy is uniquely designed to nurture talent from school level to professional level, ensuring no passionate learner is left behind.

Whether you're an O/L student, A/L student, University undergraduate, Working professional or someone planning a career switch, our academy opens doors to high-demand domains such as Data Science, Bioinformatics, Artificial Intelligence and Drug Discovery.

We’ve launched with four industry-relevant, beginner-friendly courses, each ending with a hands-on project challenge. The top three performers not only receive cash prizes but also a spot in our exclusive Talent Pool - a curated community of future data scientists. Outstanding students are offered the chance to collaborate on real-world research projects with our team, with the aim of publishing groundbreaking work and shaping global innovations.

This isn’t just about learning, it’s about creating opportunities, building futures and making an impact.

If you dream of contributing to cutting-edge research or launching a career in one of the world’s fastest-growing fields, The Data House Academy is your starting point. Join us, and let’s build a data-driven Sri Lanka and a smarter, better world.
